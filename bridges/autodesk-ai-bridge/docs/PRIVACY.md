# Privacy

Autodesk AI Bridge operates locally on Windows.

- The Bridge does not upload Revit or AutoCAD models to a separate Autodesk AI Bridge cloud service.
- Model data is sent to the configured AI/MCP client only as required for a requested operation.
- Logs remain on the local computer.
- IPC secrets are generated locally and are not intentionally logged.
- AntiGravity's own data handling, model provider, telemetry, and privacy policy are outside this project and are not changed or guaranteed by the Bridge.

Delete local logs and settings from Bridge Manager or during uninstall when prompted. Use [Security](../SECURITY.md) for vulnerability reporting.
